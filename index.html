Made the slow way.

Each piece begins at the bench — cut, shaped, soldered, hammered, filed, set and finished by hand.

Small variations, tool marks and organic textures aren't defects. They're evidence that an actual pair of hands made the thing you're wearing.

Why The Olive Feather?

The olive represents peace, hope and enduring things.

The feather is a nod to birds, the natural world, and a particularly memorable duck named Lightning.

Together, they became a fitting name for jewelry made with curiosity, patience and a little whimsy.

Hi, I'm Jacquline.

I make each Olive Feather piece in my small, home studio in Damascus, Oregon Brass is my "first love" when it comes to this craft, making enhanching each project with its strong, golden hue.
