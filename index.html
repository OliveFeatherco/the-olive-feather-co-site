Made the slow way

A little patience.
A lot of heart.

Cut, shaped, soldered, hammered, filed, set, and finished by hand. I work with brass, copper, natural stones, and reclaimed china, letting each material’s character guide the piece.

Small variations and organic textures are part of the story—jewelry made to be lived in and loved.

Meet the maker

Hi, I’m Jacquline.

I’m the hands behind The Olive Feather Co. I make each piece in my small home studio in Oregon, drawn to natural textures, old patterns, and materials with a story.

I make jewelry for people who appreciate beautiful things that don’t need to be perfect to be special.

A name with roots—and feathers

Why Olive Feather?

The olive represents peace, hope, and enduring things. The feather is a nod to the natural world and a particularly memorable duck named Lightning.

Together, they became a fitting name for jewelry made with curiosity, patience, and a little whimsy.
